# Product Context

## Why Happyness Exists

Entrepreneurs and their teams face high stress, fragmented tools, inefficient communication, and lack of reliable partners/resources. Happyness aims to solve these pains by providing an all-in-one, modular business management platform tailored for Russian SMBs.

## Problems Addressed

- Fragmented project and contractor management
- Inefficient team collaboration and communication
- Lack of trusted contractor directory and ready-to-use templates
- Time-consuming manual operations and reporting
- High cognitive load and stress for business owners
- Lack of analytics and process transparency

## User Perspective & Value

- Entrepreneurs get a single window for all business operations: user management, project tracking, contractor search/hiring, requests, payments, analytics, and reporting
- Teams collaborate in a unified environment with chat, notifications, and role-based access
- Trusted contractor base and document templates speed up operations and reduce risks
- Automated reporting and analytics for better decision-making

## User Journey Example

1. Entrepreneur registers, sets up company profile, invites team
2. Creates a new project, assigns roles, sets tasks
3. Searches for contractors in the platform directory, reviews ratings, hires
4. Manages requests and tracks project status in real-time
5. Team communicates via built-in chat, receives notifications
6. Uses templates for documents, automates reporting
7. Reviews analytics dashboard for business insights

## UX Requirements

- Fast onboarding and intuitive navigation
- Responsive, mobile-friendly UI
- Clear role separation and permissions
- Accessible, inclusive design (WCAG compliance)
- Fast search, filtering, and actionable notifications
- Consistent experience across modules
