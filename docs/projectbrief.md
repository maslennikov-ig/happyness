# Project Brief Summary

## Mission

Make 1 million people happier by 2030 by providing entrepreneurs with a comprehensive, stress-reducing business management platform.

## Vision & Core Goals

- Deliver a full-featured, modular web platform for entrepreneurs and their teams in Russia.
- Enable effective business management, time/resource savings, and personal/team well-being.
- Achieve 10M RUB/month net profit.

## Key Features

- Modular web application accessible via browser
- Core system with user/company management, authentication, role-based access
- Concierge service module for requests and tracking
- Communication (internal chat), payments, contractor directory, templates & knowledge base, dashboards, notifications, analytics
- Scalable, secure, and extensible architecture

## Target Audience

- Entrepreneurs (owners of small/medium businesses in Russia)
- Their teams (with dedicated roles)

## Project Scope

- Full-featured version in 40 weeks, no MVP: all critical modules from the start
- Integration with proven contractors, ready-to-use templates, analytics
- High reliability, security, and UX standards
